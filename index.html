About me

Serious about finance.
Not about myself.

Finance is often treated as a support function. I believe it's a growth engine — when done right.

I'm a Head of Business Finance with 5+ years of experience working at the intersection of data, strategy, and people. My work lives at the scale-up stage — where the numbers need to be sharp, the decisions need to be fast, and the teams need to stay motivated.

I don't believe finance has to be dry. The best financial leaders I've met combine rigour with curiosity, and they communicate in ways that actually make people act — not just nod along.

This site is where I share what I learn: weekly observations from the trenches, long-form thinking on topics I care about, and the occasional unpopular opinion about how business finance really works.
