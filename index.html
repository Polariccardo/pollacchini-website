Work

Problems I like to solve.

The questions leadership teams bring to me, and how I go about answering them.

“Are we ready for the next investor meeting?”
A clear narrative, the industry KPIs behind it, and an honest read against the benchmarks, ready before investors ask.
“Why did growth slow last quarter?”
Growth taken apart: cohort, retention, new business, channel and product, until the driver that changed is visible.
“Can we afford 10 more commercial hires?”
Each hire planned against the return it should bring, with clear performance expectations and a date to check them.
“How do we execute the plan?”
The financial plan broken into OKRs and milestones, then tracked with the project discipline that keeps them moving.
“Did the investment pay off?”
From the original case to the actual return: what was promised, what was delivered, and what it added in shareholder value.
“Why do Sales and Finance report different numbers?”
One source of truth, and a structured root-cause analysis every time two numbers disagree.
Thinking

What I believe.

Five principles I keep coming back to. They come from what I've seen work and fail, and they are always in progress.

  1. 01
    A method is worth 100 processes

    One shared way of working replaces a hundred separate rules.

  2. 02
    Build systems that nudge people to take the right actions

    Make the right action the easiest one to take.

  3. 03
    A tool nobody uses has no impact

    Building is the cheap part. Adoption is the real work.

  4. 04
    Complementarity is a necessary foundation for synergy

    A team adds up when its roles differ and fit together.

  5. 05
    Communicate the what, not the how

    Set the goal clearly, and let people find the way.
